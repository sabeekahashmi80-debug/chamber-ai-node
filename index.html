Three channels, one clock — not three files to reconcile

In a legacy setup, temperature, airflow, and humidity each come off a different logger, on a different clock. Lining them up afterward is manual work, and small drift compounds across a long cycle until the timeline itself is unreliable.

Ignis captures all three at the same point in time, so the overlap you see above is real, not reconstructed — the zero-drift result of point-of-capture fusion rather than post-hoc alignment.

Most thermal tools point at one or the other. Ignis fuses them, because a passed part in a failing chamber is still a bad result.

PUT diagnostics

Watches the device itself for the early signs that precede a failure, not just the failure.

  • Flags thermal runaway on individual components before it cascades
  • Tracks solder joint stress under vibration or rapid cycling
  • Maps thermal hotspots to the fault codes they produce
