The Infrastructure Deficit: Why Traditional Monitors Fail

The Legacy Status Quo

Standard loggers sit passively inside a chamber box. If a seal breaks, they simply record the ambient temperature drop. They cannot tell you *why* it dropped—forcing engineers to spent hours debugging whether the heating assembly broke, the door was open, or the product failed.

This manual trial-and-error cycle risks invalidating weeks of profile data data logging, stalling high-stakes qualification timelines.

The Ignis Advantage

By integrating multispectral imaging with edge AI at the point of capture, Ignis separates the environment data from the hardware data. If a gasket fails, the system triggers an anomaly event pinpointing the structural coordinates of the leak immediately.

You get instant, audit-ready data lineage that proves your testing environment remained perfectly uniform throughout the entire compliance execution block.
